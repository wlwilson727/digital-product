# Low-cost demand validation and acquisition plan

All activity below is prepared, not executed. No ads, unsolicited messages, emails or public listings have been sent/published. Initial validation ceiling is $1,000; planned spend is not authorization.

## Stage 1: problem evidence ($0 new spend)

After approved source-domain networking is applied, review five competitor listings per selected niche and record advertised price/date/format/reviews without assuming sales. Use owner-approved channels to invite five consented independent service-business interviews. Ask about the last rate decision, scope overrun or payment shortfall; identify current alternatives and time/cash consequences. Avoid collecting private client finances. Gate: at least three of five describe a recent concrete problem and an existing workaround. This sample is directional, not statistically representative. No gate currently passed.

## Stage 2: comprehension and value ($0–$100 proposed)

With approved recruitment, observe five testers using fictional scenarios. Ask them to explain inputs/outputs and export a decision. Gate: four of five complete core task without intervention; no material calculation misunderstanding; median support under ten minutes. Optional incentives require explicit spending approval and must not buy favorable reviews. Test two price propositions ($39 and $49) only with transparent actual offer terms; survey preference is not purchase demand.

## Stage 3: paid pilot (up to $250 advertising + approved store costs)

Owner approves listing, publication, refunds and precise spend before action. Choose one intent audience and one product; do not split a tiny budget across many channels. Measure unique eligible sessions, completed paid orders, refunds, CAC, contribution and support minutes. Target five unrelated paying customers at the intended price as a minimal pilot signal; no friends/family revenue presented as general demand. Define stopping rule before spending: stop at approved cap or after repeated tracking/fulfillment errors. If 200 eligible sessions produce zero orders, investigate offer, trust, price and traffic relevance; zero orders does not prove absence of demand everywhere. Do not scale from a handful of orders.

## Acquisition experiments

| Channel | Prepared experiment | Metric and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify SEO | Original page on billable utilization with fictional worked example and product link | Search impressions → eligible sessions → sales; weeks/months possible; no traffic guarantee |
| Search landing pages | Pricing, scope contingency and weekly cash timing each get a distinct explanation | Conversion by intent; avoid thin duplicate pages |
| Etsy discovery | Verify digital/AI disclosure policy; prepare original download listing | Marketplace conversion/fees; saturation unknown; account/publication approval required |
| Pinterest | Original scenario chart and useful worksheet preview | Tagged clicks and purchases; do not count impressions as demand |
| Organic social | Demonstrate one input changing an output with no earnings promise | Qualified visits and orders; posting approval required |
| Professional communities | Seek moderator-approved educational contribution | Relevant problem evidence; no unsolicited promotional spam |
| Affiliates | Prepare terms and contribution cap before recruiting | Reconciled net sales; agreement/payment approval required |
| Email | Draft opt-in tips with unsubscribe mechanism | Consent, click and sales attribution; no purchased lists |
| Bundles | Show connected pricing/scope/cash workflow | AOV and contribution, not AOV alone |
| Paid search | Narrow intent keyword test after approved exact cap | Measured CAC vs contribution; not assumed cheap |
