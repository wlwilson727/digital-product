## Shopify integration plan for later approval

Prepare reviewed product copy and unpublished CSV rows, attach release ZIP through chosen approved app, set digital/no-shipping behavior and test receipt/download access. Bundles may require delivering a dedicated combined ZIP or an app with verified component fulfillment. Discounts are store features to configure only after approval; upsell apps introduce cost/privacy risk and are unnecessary for a first pilot. SEO preparation includes clear title, helpful body copy, accessible previews, descriptive URLs and internal links. Customer communications should use approved receipt/documentation text; marketing email needs consent and applicable unsubscribe rules.

For analytics, export orders/refunds/campaign spend and store sessions with matching periods/currency. Reconcile gross orders, discounts, refund totals, taxes, fees and payout timing; never label modeled processing fees actual. Track refunds by product and reason without storing identifying data in Git. Automated delivery does not automate tax/legal compliance or exception handling.

No access is requested until the owner approves concrete draft integration. Account plan/app inventories and scopes must be inspected first. A prepared CSV is not a published Shopify listing; fulfillment attachment and test checkout remain manual/integration tasks. Compare alternatives on total cost and incremental qualified customers, not transaction fees alone.
