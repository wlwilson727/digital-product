# Operating boundaries

The owner authorized independent research, original product development, local tests, documentation, listing preparation, financial modeling, and commits to an authorized development branch. Their supplied assignment is the project brief. No healthcare, hospital, Epic, or healthcare consulting products are permitted. No employment materials may be used.

Explicit approval remains necessary before spending, subscriptions, domain registration, opening seller accounts, any live Shopify changes or installations, publishing, advertisements, outreach, contracts, paid deployment, recurring charges, or protected-branch merges. No such action is part of the local build.

Prepared listings must remain unpublished drafts. Existing store access has not been provided. Never put customer records, credentials, or private financial records in Git. Store local business CSVs outside the repository or in ignored `private-data/`; browser data remains local to that browser origin. A future integration needs scoped access and retention rules.

These tools run only when invoked. They do not constitute a continuously running autonomous service. Financial objectives and business assumptions are targets, never revenue promises.
