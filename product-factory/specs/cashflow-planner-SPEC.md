# Specification: Cash Runway Planner 1.0.0

Customer: Small service businesses and independent operators

Problem: Understand thirteen-week cash timing and identify projected shortfalls.

## Required behavior

- Thirteen-week cash projection
- Weekly receipts and payments
- Cash warning indicators
- CSV export
- Offline use

## Acceptance gates

- Implement a dependency-free, offline browser tool with validated inputs.
- Independently verify calculations and edge cases with tests.
- Include guide, FAQ, single-business license and preview.
- Validate responsive layout, keyboard use and exports manually.
- Use fictional examples and explain financial assumptions.
- Package only explicit manifest-approved files.
- Demand remains unvalidated until approved customer experiments provide evidence.
- Owner approves all publication and spending.
