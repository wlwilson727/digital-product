# Product catalog

All products are **unpublished, pending owner review, commercially unvalidated**. `product-factory/catalog.json` is the machine-readable manifest; `releases/catalog.json` links actual packaged artifacts and their hashes. Names and feature detail come from those files and each customer guide.

| Product directory | Customer decision | Proposed test price | Package |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `products/service-pricing/` | Price service work using costs, available billable capacity, and financial targets | $39 | `releases/service-pricing-1.0.0.zip` |
| `products/cashflow-planner/` | Anticipate cash shortfalls over thirteen weeks | $49 | `releases/cashflow-planner-1.0.0.zip` |
| `products/project-estimator/` | Estimate project effort, risk, costs and scope | $39 | `releases/project-estimator-1.0.0.zip` |

Proposed bundle: $89 for all three, subject to owner review and demand evidence. No bundle checkout or discount is configured. These prices are hypotheses, not competitor observations or appraised values.

Each package includes the offline application, calculation code, guide, FAQ, customer license, and SVG preview. Planning tools use fictional examples and USD assumptions. They are not accounting, tax, investment or legal advice. Do not bundle internal research, the dashboard, or private operating data into customer downloads.

Future recommendations include service retainers/capacity, industry profitability, and seasonal cash reserves. Build only after permitted demand evidence and customer observations justify additional scope. The manifest and factory can package future original browser tools without rebuilding the pipeline.
