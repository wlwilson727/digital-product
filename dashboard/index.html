    Definitions, CSV format, and data controls

    Each row represents one product in a reporting period. Period totals are additive: do not import overlapping totals. ID must be unique. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Currency is USD; do not mix currencies.

    Gross = price × orders. Refunds, fees, advertising, delivery, production and fixed costs are dollar totals for that row, not rates. Fees include payment and marketplace fees. Net revenue = gross − refunds − fees. Contribution = net revenue − ads − delivery. Estimated operating profit = contribution − production − fixed. Production is expensed once in the relevant period; allocate fixed costs once across products to avoid double counting. Taxes and owner distributions are excluded.

    Conversion = orders ÷ visits; visits means storefront sessions measured consistently. CAC = ads ÷ new customers, not all orders; count each acquired customer once. Without customer identity or attribution, manually validate customer counts across products. Zero denominators display “—”.

    CSV imports must use the downloaded template headers exactly. Required statuses: development planned/building/ready, approval pending/approved/rejected, listing draft/live/archived. Status labels are owner-supplied, never proof of publication. Set a status by exporting, editing the CSV and importing it. No external action occurs.

    CSV spreadsheet formula prefixes are neutralized on export; resulting leading apostrophes may remain on reimport. Use this dashboard only for aggregate internal data. Synthetic values are examples, not evidence of sales or recommended accounting treatments.
