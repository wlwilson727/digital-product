Start here

  1. Extract the entire ZIP. Keep the folders together. Open this START-HERE.html file in a current desktop browser.
  2. Open each tool and load its fictional example. Replace the sample assumptions before relying on the results.
  3. Export editable JSON after each session. CSV is a report, not a backup of editable inputs. Print / save PDF uses your browser's print dialog.

Connect the tools without counting margin twice

Use Service Price Lab's cost per billable hour as the labor cost rate in Project Scope Studio. Do not carry the target selling rate into that field unless you deliberately intend to add another margin. Enter project-specific direct costs once.

Enter the estimate's deposit and balance into Cash Runway Planner in the weeks you expect payment to reach your bank. Enter expenses when cash will leave. Owner-pay targets in the pricing tool do not automatically become cash payments.

These tools do not sync. Transfer figures manually and review them. The project estimator does not include payment fees; allow for those separately when reconciling a price.

A worked example

A fictional studio has $20,000 annual overhead and $60,000 desired take-home pay, with a 25% owner tax reserve. At 40 hours a week, 50 weeks and 50% billable time, its 1,000 billable hours carry $100 cost per hour.

A 10-hour project with $100 direct costs costs $1,100. In Project Scope Studio, a 10% contingency and 20% margin produce a $1,512.50 quote before any discount. A 40% deposit is $605; the remaining balance is $907.50.

For the cash schedule, assume $2,000 opening cash and a $1,000 reserve. If the $605 deposit arrives in week one and $700 of payments leave, closing cash is $1,905. If the $907.50 balance arrives in week two and $400 leaves, closing cash is $2,412.50. These are invented figures, not market benchmarks.

Weekly review

  1. Compare actual project hours with your estimate and revise future assumptions.
  2. Move delayed receipts to their expected collection week. Check the lowest cash balance and reserve gap.
  3. Save dated JSON backups for each tool; protect files containing client or financial information.
