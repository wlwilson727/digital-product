Before you use the results

All amounts use one selected currency. No conversion is performed. Figures are planning estimates and exclude sales tax/VAT unless you explicitly include it in your costs. Read the customer guide for formulas, workflow and limitations. Data stays in this browser; exported files may contain sensitive business information.
