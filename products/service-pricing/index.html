Capacity and annual costs

Your pricing targets

Owner pay is treated as a cost, profit is additional surplus. This model assumes you sell all planned billable capacity.

Put the estimate to work

  1. Use actual annual costs and realistic billable time.
  2. Compare the indicated job price with customer willingness to pay.
  3. If the market will not support the price, improve scope, costs or utilization rather than silently lowering your pay target.

Before you use the results

All amounts use one selected currency. No conversion is performed. Figures are planning estimates and exclude sales tax/VAT unless you explicitly include it in your costs. Read the customer guide for formulas, workflow and limitations. Data stays in this browser; exported files may contain sensitive business information.

Empty number fields are interpreted as zero; enter all assumptions deliberately. No account, internet connection or subscription needed. Fictional examples are illustrations, not benchmarks or financial advice.
